Questions & answers

Things people ask
before they join.

  • What exactly is Owlry?+

    Owlry is a book discovery secretary — an app that helps you find the right book for your mood, moment, and ambition. Instead of endless browsing, you describe what you're looking for and Owlry fetches it. Think of it as a personal librarian who actually knows you.

  • Is the free seat actually free?+

    Yes. The free seat gives you access to Owlry at launch with no payment required. Ever. You'll get discovery, previews, and a basic reading tracker — no credit card, no trial period, no catch. Founding seats are for those who want to lock in a lifetime rate and shape what we build.

  • When does Owlry launch?+

    We're not announcing a date yet — we'd rather get it right than get it out. Readers on the waitlist will be the first to know, and founding seat holders get access before anyone else. Sign up and we'll write to you when Owlry launches.

  • How is this different from Goodreads or StoryGraph?+

    Those are tracking and rating tools. Owlry is a discovery tool — the focus is on finding your next book, not cataloguing what you've read. You can ask anything ("something quiet for a Sunday," "a book that will make me think differently about money") and Owlry gives you a curated answer, not a list of 3,000 results.

  • What does a founding seat include?+

    A $7.97 one-time payment locks in $9.90/mo for life — even after we raise prices post-launch. You also get opening-night access before the free tier, a founding member badge on your Reading Identity, and a vote on what we build next. There are only 200 founding seats, and they're going.

  • What happens to my data and my shelf?+

    Your reading is yours. We don't sell your data, train models on your library, or share anything with third parties. Your shelf is private by default. We take the same view of your reading life as a good librarian: discreet, respectful, and entirely in your service.

  • I'm not a big reader. Is Owlry for me?+

    Especially yes. Owlry is built for people who want to read more but don't know where to start — not people who already have a towering pile of books. If you finish one great book because Owlry put it in your hands, that's a win. Owlry is for the curious, not just the avid.
